This is the first post. It exists so the site is not empty on launch day, and to show what a post looks like. Replace its content with your real first piece, or delete the file.

A post is a text file in the `content/posts/` folder. The three lines between the dashes at the top (title and date) are the only rules. Everything else is free text.

## What you can do in a post

Paragraphs, obviously. Links, like [this one to Martin Fowler's site](https://martinfowler.com). **Bold** and *italic*. Quotes:

> The best design for long-form reading is the one you completely forget about.

Subheadings with `##`. Lists if you really must. And videos, by pasting the YouTube embed code as is:

<iframe src="https://www.youtube-nocookie.com/embed/dQw4w9WgXcQ" title="Video" allow="fullscreen" allowfullscreen></iframe>

The video player adapts to the screen width on its own, phones included.
